Congratulations to [Junhao Zhao](/_members/junhao-zhao) for having his poster "When Model Complexity Matters in Cross-Subject Naturalistic fMRI Prediction" accepted for the **2026 IEEE BRAIN Discovery and Neurotechnology Workshop**! Junhao also received a travel award for complimentary accomodation as he presents at the workshop. The preprint can be found [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04041).

We congratulate Junhao on his achievement and look forward to his future work!
